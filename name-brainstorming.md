# Platform Name Brainstorming

## Current Frontrunner

### So Far So Good
- Inspired by the La Haine quote: "A man falls from a 50 story building. Every floor he passes he says: so far so good, so far so good, so far so good. It's not about how you fall. It's about how you land."
- Captures the philosophy: we're falling through the universe with our eyes open, learning on the way down
- Not fake optimism, not doom — lucid, clear-eyed wonder
- Universal phrase everyone already knows
- No one uses it in the science/education space
- Concern: doesn't immediately communicate "science platform" — needs a tagline to bridge the gap (e.g., "So Far So Good — a science commons")
- Domain options: sofarsogood.app (~$15-20/yr), .dev, .xyz, .org — avoid .science ($300+ premium)

## Strong Contenders

### In2Theory
- Read aloud: "in theory" — casual, inviting
- Read visually: "into theory" — diving in, exploring
- Also: "I'm into theory" — identity, passion
- The "2" acts as a natural visual separator
- Great for URLs and handles

### Into The Theory
- More cinematic/immersive than In Theory
- "Into the wild" / "into the void" energy
- Implies depth — going *into* it, not skimming

### In Theory
- Casual and disarming
- Literally about theory
- Slightly self-aware, a little funny
- Instantly memorable

## What the Platform Feels Like

Based on conversation about influences and values:

- **La Haine** — the fall is the point, eyes open, "so far so good"
- **Dr. Stone** — science as adventure, rebuilding understanding from scratch, making science fun
- **Core interests:** black holes, special relativity, human anatomy, the brain, entropy, EUV lithography, machine learning
- **The tension:** the body breaks down but the mind deepens; time is cruel but gives meaning
- **Vibe:** not a lecture, not a textbook — a journey. Communal but you can be a passenger. Hopefully leads to discoveries.
- **Tone:** lucid, honest, slightly dark, slightly funny, slightly brave

## Key Naming Insight

Two philosophies:
1. **Name that explains itself** (Atlas, In2Theory) — immediate clarity
2. **Name that means something deeper** (So Far So Good) — earns its explanation, builds a stronger brand, but takes patience and a good tagline
